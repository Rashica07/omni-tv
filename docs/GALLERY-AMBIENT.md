# OMNI Gallery: ambient display and gallery

Two modes in one module (`android/apps/gallery`). **Ambient** is a full-screen photograph with an oversized clock; **Gallery** (Photos / Videos) is a quiet thumbnail grid with a full-screen viewer.

**Photograph sources** (all behind the `ImageProvider` idea, so any can be replaced): Bing's daily photograph (`BingProvider`), open-licence landscapes from Wikimedia Commons, and this TV's own photos. Bing: the feed `www.bing.com/HPImageArchive.aspx` is the one Bing's home page uses and is **not a documented API**; it is isolated in `BingFeed` (pure parsing, tested) and `BingProvider`. It is asked at most twice a day (not within 12 h of a good reading, not within 30 min of a failure), downloads only the 3 newest at 1920x1080, never an image twice, keeps the last 10 days on disk and keeps showing them offline. It carries the title and description Bing wrote, the photographer's credit and the date; nothing is invented, and a location is shown only as part of Bing's own description (the feed has no separate location field). Images are personal-use, credited to their photographers. No key or secret is involved.

**The clock** (`ClockView`): Geist (bundled), thin by default ("minimal") or heavier with a soft translucent gradient and a trace of depth ("expressive"); no outline. Placement (`ClockPlacement`, pure and tested) looks at a 32x18 luminance grid of each photograph and puts the clock in its quietest region (centre, left, right, top or bottom) so the subject stays clear, with dark ink on a bright region and ivory on a dark one; a soft gradient appears only on the clock's side and only for light ink. The credit moves to the top when the clock takes the bottom. The position can also be fixed in the settings. Digits fade up on a minute change.

**Motion**: a 1.4 s cross-fade and a slow 6% drift over the time a photograph is on screen. Still when the system's animations are off, on low-RAM TVs or with Motion set to Still. One bitmap at screen size per photograph; thumbnails are about 360 px in a 6 MB cache.

**Automatic**: Gallery settings, "Start when idle" (Never by default; 5/10/30 min). The launcher starts the ambient display after that long without a remote key while it is on screen; it does not start over a menu, the setup, the assistant, or (if "While music plays" says so) while something is playing. Any key leaves it. The ambient display holds the screen on for the chosen hours only (1/2/4/8, default 2); after that the TV's own power management takes over. This is OMNI's own full-screen activity and the screensaver, **not** a lock screen. If the TV never gives the launcher focus, there is no idle detection: Settings -> "Ambient display" starts it explicitly.

**Music**: while a media session plays, a small artwork and title capsule shows at the bottom right (needs the notification-listener switch; see NOW-PLAYING.md). Music is never touched.

**Gallery**: Photos (Bing's today first, then the rest) and Videos (this TV's, opened in the system player), 3 large thumbnails across, focus ring and title on the focused one, a viewer with Left/Right, OK for the credit, Up for favourites. Settings are one page of option cards.
